  missingFromAll:
   - Enzyme and alkali supply limit. soils section 9 (condition 3) says the film model must provide a liquid-side mass-transfer or adsorption term, 'not just wetted', and attributes the thesis model's two failed in-machine cases to enzyme delivery. No proposal has this on by default: A and B relax film composition towards the liquor and track fresh fraction; C offers a cap on u_r that is switched off. Local consumption of enzyme and bleach under a slow film is dropped by all three.
   - Erosion bookkeeping on unswollen soil. soils E6b erodes a thickness u_r and deletes the top cell when E_r exceeds its current thickness. Where water supply limits swelling the cell is thin, so the same u_r removes dry mass about 5.6 times faster than from a swollen layer (1.1e-4 against 1.95e-5 kg m-2 s-1 for the egg-yolk tile). All three implement the rule as written; with 'bead = wet' (A, B) the worst-wetted soil is cleaned fastest. Erosion must be mass-based or gated on local hydration.

With the law as specified none of the three will reproduce those sizes, and no proposal notes the conflict between soils 6.2 (removal homogeneous over the tile, jet share about 10 %) and the checklist. A loosened-layer law or a renewal-limited rate has to be decided before the checklist is used as a gate.

The brief allows resolved 3D for exactly this, and all three say that wire, rim, vessel-interior and sweeping-footprint closures should come from offline resolved runs (jets section 10 gives the domain: 60 mm at 50 micrometres for 50 ms), but none specifies the runs, the table formats, or the test that the analytic chain must pass against them. Every proposal therefore ships the same [ESTIMATED] rules for the cases the jets dossier calls its largest gaps (known gaps 3, 4, 5).
   - The time scales between a revolution and a phase in the film solvers. Only C treats wetting onset, the unsprayed rack's drain-down over the 45-60 s diverter dwell and late drips; A and B sample the unsprayed rack 5-9 s after the switch and apply that to the whole dwell. Even C does this with a model that has no film dynamics. The merged design needs these slow-state mechanisms on top of a film PDE, which nobody proposed.
   - Statistical convergence of the wetting map. A records one revolution, B one or two, C one or two (with dense parcels but a decoupled film). No proposal shows that the per-cell wetting class, the quantity that enters every soil mechanism as a switch, is converged, and none defines a per-cell (not per-item) noise estimate that would stop a single splash from deciding 47 minutes of soil integration.
   - Viscous dewetting and surface age. The films dossier's own recommended contact-line model (12.4) contains no viscous limit although 5.5 says the smaller of the viscous and inertial speeds governs; A and B inherit the gap and C replaces it with a clock using an invented 10 mm length. None handles dynamic surface tension at surface ages of 0.01-1 s (films section 13 item 2): all switch abruptly from the clean-water value in the jet zone to the equilibrium liquor value and its contact angles in the slow film.
   - Air in the tub. All three assume still, uniform air. Splatter droplets of 0.1-0.3 mm stop within 5-15 cm (jets 3.7) and are then carried by whatever recirculation the arms and jets drive; the diffuse wetting on which the negative controls P16, P24, P28 and P44 rest depends on it. No mist transport, no spatially resolved condensation or drying air flow.
   - Particle transport and where specks land. P21 expects more than half of otherwise clean items to carry specks when spinach is in the load, preferentially on upward faces, in slow zones and in cavities. All three carry particles as lumped liquor classes plus a per-cell intensity from invented capture or stranding laws; none tracks particles through films, pools and the filter, so the spatial pattern is a guess. Flakes (P34) are mass, not bodies.
   - Two-way coupling for thick or rough soil. Berms that deflect the film, jets burrowing and blistering thick deposits (soils section 9, condition 2), soil roughness raising wall shear (jets 4.3: 5-28 micrometre roughness raises stagnation transfer by up to 50 %) and the wavy surface of pre-wetted starch are absent from all three; only contact angle feeds back.
   - The chosen machine and programme. None of the budgets is worked for the Bosch SMV4HAX40G on Auto 45-65 for 145 minutes (A and B predate the lead's note; C names an AUTO class but sizes ECO 50). That case has a cold pre-rinse (a clean-water wetting class), a main wash up to 65 C where enzyme denaturation and fat melting are first-order, a tablet falling into a tray on the upper basket, and drying by a side-wall heat exchanger, which needs a wall-temperature and condensation model that no proposal describes beyond lumped coefficients.
   - Run-to-run variability (P46) does not emerge in any design; each can only tune seeded inputs to hit 0.08-0.10. Aerated jets and pump surge under starvation or foam are reduced to a head factor in all three. Capillary rise in gaps and at contacts is a static store with an invented exchange coefficient (0.1 in A, 0.02 in B, zero in C).
   - Validation of wetting before calibration. All three wetting models rest on one data set (cast acrylic, water at 43 C, a 3.8 mm reference grid) and on receding angles the films dossier rates as uncertain by +/- 20 degrees. No proposal says how the wetted fraction on real glazed, steel and plastic tableware in wash liquor will be checked before the soil results are believed, although every soil mechanism is switched by it.
   - A determinism probe suite per class of operation and the full stack string in cache keys and golden tests, as required by the lead's note of 19:50 after the operating-system update; C comes closest, none has the probe suite.

######## SYNTHESIS ########
## summary
The contract is written to docs/design/sim-core.md (2,161 lines); no other repository file was touched. No kernel of this design has been run, so every time below is a planning figure until milestone MS0 measures the real kernels.

The design starts from Proposal C's pipeline, which two of three judges ranked first: compute exposure records per periodic spray regime, then replay every arm revolution of the programme against them. It is the only structure whose hydrodynamic cost does not grow with the owner's 145-minute Auto programme, and the only one with slow-state physics, a bounded worst case and a cache that makes calibration affordable. Onto it I grafted A's geometry and inertial film solver, and B's exposure content and peel integral.

One wash runs in three layers:

- **Exposure.** For each (diverter state, pump class, liquor class):
  - Jets are traced against exact analytic geometry at 16,384 arm phases; the jets dossier's closed forms are gathered onto 1 mm soil cells.
  - Diffuse secondary water is a Monte Carlo of up to 4 x 10^8 parcels per diverter state into phase-binned integer tables.
  - Surface water is time-marched by two solvers behind one contract:
    - S, an inertial depth-averaged film solver (MUSCL, two-stage SSP, hysteretic contact line), runs on hollow ware and all partially wetting items.
    - K, C's kinematic cascade, runs on glazed flat ware, mesh patches, tub and wires.
  - K then runs a 60 s drain-down for every state.
- **Replay.** The 12-layer soil model is stepped once per revolution (about 3,430 ticks) with a slow-state layer: wetting-onset gate, drain-down merge after each diverter switch, pools, wall temperatures, film composition, condensation and redeposition. There is no cycle jumping.
- **Events.** The load is shaken down with cheap jet loads before any exposure is computed. Later motion opens a geometry epoch with a bounded re-exposure. Exposure sets are computed lazily in programme order, so each sees the then-current poses and soil-dependent contact angles; this keeps A's "real predecessor state" without A's window growth.

Where the judges disagreed on the film model I chose the numerics judge's allocation. The physics judge wanted the inertial solver everywhere; the delivery judge wanted it only where a gate fails. The chosen split puts the PDE where the cascade's dropped terms are first order and its one-dimensional evidence does not reach, and keeps the cascade where a kinematic wave is the correct leading-order model. A pre-agreed table upgrades plates to S, or downgrades to K only, from the MS0 kernel measurement and the plate/inverted-bowl gate.

Corrections that go beyond the proposals:

- **Momentum-flux factor.** It is 1.2 on both friction branches. I checked that neither the dossier's switch nor a smooth blend is grid-converged in pulsed flow in a 1-D first-order test.
- **Dewetting and contact line.** A viscous cap limits the retraction force, and beta_cl follows a tilt law that fits both acrylic windows.
- **Soil.** Erosion is mass-based with a hydration gate, and an enzyme/alkali supply factor is on by default, normalised to the in-machine tile. State is f32.
- **Peel term.** It is the integral of the momentum-flux exceedance curve.
- **Arm friction.** Re-tuned for C_v = 0.97 (8.6, 3.6, 4.4 mm).
- **Standards against soils.** On the size of the jet effect on protein I sided with the soils dossier's measurements.

The document also resolves each "missing from all" point with a design or a justified deferral, including a specified offline closure campaign, a determinism probe suite with stack strings, a representation-independent result contract, and a drying node for the side-wall heat exchanger.

Official tier on the Auto 145 sizing case: 16.4 minutes for a 6.1 m2 load and 20.2 minutes for the 8.5 m2 design load. With every reserve drawn the bound is 22.4 and 27.5 minutes. GPU kernel time is 13.6 to 17.2 minutes against the 20-minute allowance, and peak GPU memory is 1.6 GB. The fast tier takes 2.4 to 2.8 minutes with the same solver allocation.

The sizing machine is still SPOT-60 data with a stretched Auto realisation, because the Bosch dossier does not exist yet.

## decisions
 - D1 Starting point: Proposal C's expose-then-replay pipeline (ranked first by the numerics and delivery judges). Why: hydrodynamic cost does not grow with the 145-minute programme; it has slow-state physics, a bounded worst case and an exposure cache. A's programme march is rejected as the structure, but its ordering is kept through lazy, programme-ordered exposure sets.
 - D2 Surface representation: A's generator-following structured patches (closed-meridian (s, theta) patch with power-of-two ragged rows and pole cells; Cartesian patches), with B's in-plane-subdivided triangle patches only for hull and scaled parts, line elements for wires and gutters, and one cell-graph view (delivery judge). Why: exact normals at grazing incidence, rim and both faces in one grid, O(1) hit-to-cell map, and a match to the platform's analytic primitives. B's remeshing pipeline is rejected.
 - D3 Resolutions: 2 mm blocks; film cells 2 mm on glass and glazed ware and 1 mm on partially wetting materials; 1 mm soil cells with a cover fraction (C; numerics and delivery judges against the physics judge's 0.5 mm). Why: standards 10.5 needs 1 to 4 mm2 with sub-element coverage; 0.5 mm in f32 costs four times the state and moves to the reference tier.
 - D4 Geometry queries: exact analytic primitives in a two-level structure with (r, z) half-plane trees (A, C), wavefront parcels, and the measured triangle BVH with analytic polish as the pre-agreed fallback. Why: unmeasured, so MS0 benchmarks it with a go/no-go.
 - D5 Jets: 16,384 phases and 19 sub-rays (C); per-soil-cell gather in developed coordinates with one fold crossing (A), integrated along each track segment (B); hand-over at min(R_j, r*, edge, validity bound) through A's retarded ring emitter carrying momentum; wrenches from A's momentum theorem. Rejected: B's 2,048 phases and fixed-speed hand-over, C's relaxation-length offset.
 - D6 Secondary water split by source (physics judge): C's Monte Carlo into phase-binned integer tables for diffuse primary sources, with smoothing masked by face and normal; A's time-coupled parcels for everything the film emits. Why: wetting of shadowed faces is decided at 1e-3 to 1e-4 of the mean flux, where A and B have under one parcel per cell.
 - D7 Surface water: two solvers behind one contract. S (A's inertial film with the judges' fixes: MUSCL, two-stage SSP, CFL 0.3 with capillary celerity, donor-local positivity cap, blended branch for G_n <= 0) on hollow ware and all partially wetting structured items. K (C's cascade with the regime machine corrected) on glazed flat ware, mesh patches, tub, wires and every drain-down. Allocation from the numerics judge, against the physics judge (S everywhere) and the delivery judge (S only where a gate fails); a pre-agreed variant table decides S-all, S-split or K-only at MS0/MS3.
 - D8 Momentum-flux factor beta_m = 1.2 on both friction branches until a pulsed-film reference exists (new, from the delivery judge's finding). Why: with the dossier's 1.2/1.016 switch, and also with a smooth blend, a 1-D first-order test is not grid-converged in pulsed flow; with 1.2 it converges and agrees with the cascade within 9 %.
 - D9 Wetting closures: the films 12.4 contact-line model plus a viscous cap on the retraction force (films 5.5), a tilt law for beta_cl fitted to both acrylic windows with one entry per cell size, bead-covered fraction instead of 'any bead is wet', a tilt-independent minimum wetting rate and a fixed 4 mm regime scale in the cascade (C, physics and numerics judges).
 - D10 Exposure records: C's three-record structure with B's content. FilmExposure is 32 B with low-shear duty levels at 0.5 to 4 Pa and a transient/steady label (A); DrainDown is 8 B; JetExposure is 56 B with exceedance curves in shear (including 10 Pa) and in momentum flux to 32 N/m, peaks, four ordered events and longest rest. The peel term is B's integral of the momentum exceedance curve.
 - D11 Whole-cycle integration: replay every revolution (C), no cycle jumping; exposure sets computed lazily in programme order with then-current poses, pools and soil-dependent contact angles (A's intent); each diverter state starts from the periodic state of the other so the switch transient is recorded; 60 s drain-down merge, onset gate, pool ODEs, condensation (C's slow-state layer).
 - D12 Soil coupling: soils section 7 on f32 state with C's kinetics choice (soils set plus the chemistry dossier's enzyme denaturation optimum). New: mass-based erosion gated on local hydration, and an enzyme/alkali/oxidant supply factor on by default, normalised to the in-machine tile. On the standards-versus-soils conflict over jet share I sided with the soils measurements; a loosened-layer law is specified but off.
 - D13 Rigid bodies: C's shake-down before exposure, live jet loads re-cast each engine step, geometry epochs with bounded re-exposure; Jolt at 1/240 s through the platform PhysicsWorld; arms as one-degree-of-freedom bodies and the tablet spawned at dispenser time (A, physics judge). B's open-loop wrench series is rejected.
 - D14 Determinism: the union of the three rule sets plus the full stack string in every key and hash, operating-system update as a version bump, reference stack and dead band for verdicts, a per-operation-class probe suite, ordered accumulator clears, integer block reductions, no narrow accumulators, resume-reproduces-hash, and the platform's @spotless/core generator instead of Threefry or pcg4d.
 - D15 Budget use: no closure bracket inside scored runs (numerics and delivery judges against C). The minutes go to the inertial solver, dense parcels, transient records and drain-downs; bracket and sigma replay are developer options on cached exposure. Reserves are bounded by a policy chosen from pre-run work prediction, never from the clock.
 - D16 Hydraulics: C_v = 0.97 (jets) with the machine dossier's per-nozzle C_d, and arm friction radii re-tuned to 8.6, 3.6 and 4.4 mm so the arms keep 35, 30 and 60 rpm. No arm speed is snapped for periodicity (integer phase accumulators at true speeds). Pump speeds are grouped in +/- 4 % classes, narrowed from C's +/- 8 % after the delivery judge's splatter objection.

## openIssues
 - No kernel of this design has been measured. The budget rests on planning rates (S at 1.0 G stage-updates/s, K at 0.30 G/s, parcels at 90 M segments/s, analytic tracing at 60 M/s, soil at 0.20 G/s). The S-all / S-split / K-only choice is deferred to MS0 and MS3 by a pre-agreed table.
 - The physics judge wanted the inertial film on every surface; the planned official tier keeps the cascade on glazed flat ware (62 % of area) unless the plate gate fails and the S kernel reaches 0.8 G/s. The cascade's two-dimensional adequacy on plates is still untested.
 - Pulsed transitional films: beta_m = 1.2 on both friction branches contradicts the films dossier's 1.016 on the turbulent branch. The supporting evidence is a one-dimensional, first-order, vertical test; no measured or resolved pulsed-film reference exists (campaign C5 is specified, not run).
 - With beta_m = 1.2 the Courant number on the fastest wave is 0.3 to 0.51 under the films 12.6 time-step rule. Stability at production settings, and a level monitor that can only raise a level from the next phase bin, must be confirmed at MS3.
 - The enzyme/alkali supply factor is on by default as the physics judge required, but its uptake constant is this document's estimate and is confounded with the Hill fit made on the same six in-machine curves. It sets the size of P02 and threatens the negative controls P16, P24, P28 and P44.
 - Standards P02/P30 against soils 6.1/6.2: I sided with the soils measurements (jet share about 10 %, linear in duty). P02 for protein is gated on sign only until the paired-tile calibration test is run; the loosened-layer law is specified but off.
 - Secondary-water closures (splatter factor 0 to 1, wire and rim rules, splash ratio, droplet-train efficiency) remain estimates that decide the wetting of shadowed faces. The offline closure campaign is specified (cases, method, table formats, acceptance test) but has no executed runs, hardware or schedule.
 - The delivery judge asked for Monte Carlo deposition per pump speed. The design uses +/- 4 % pump classes, leaving up to 17 % error in the splatter source, and rescales the water-like sets by 11 % for about two minutes at the start of the main wash. The intermediate rinse is treated as water-like.
 - Soil-driven wetting feedback: an exposure set is computed once per geometry epoch. Cascade cells are re-evaluated per tick, but S-solved cells only raise a wettingDrift flag; the predictor-corrector refresh the delivery judge suggested exists only in the reference tier.
 - Cutlery (47 % of items) runs the cascade on triangle patches with a tangent-plane fan and no capillary term: the least accurate geometry. Per-shape structured generators for spoon bowls and blades are a fallback, not planned work.
 - Not modelled: air motion and mist transport (zero-field hook only), particle tracking and flakes as bodies, two-way coupling of thick soil, dynamic surface tension at short surface ages, emergent run-to-run variability (P46), entrainment of residual film by the fast film.
 - Monte Carlo wall states come from a preview-tier pilot and are not iterated in the official tier. Until a basin is full its overflow drips are over-counted. States shorter than 120 s per programme (state A) are superposed, not time-marched.
 - The Bosch SMV4HAX40G dossier does not exist yet. Nozzle tables, pump speeds, diverter pattern, arm count, tablet-tray geometry and heat-exchanger size are SPOT-60 values or placeholders, and AUTO-145 is a stretched realisation of the machine dossier's Auto programme.
 - Platform dependencies: @spotless/physics needs two additions (changing mass properties in place, contact forces on kinematic bodies per step), and the MachineSpec and programme types in @spotless/machine are still an empty skeleton.
 - Cost depends on the material mix: a load dominated by plastics or steel (1 mm inertial class) would exceed the budget. This is handled by a level-design lint and per-item overrides, which needs confirming as acceptable.
 - Several wetting and soil constants are this document's own estimates with no data behind them: dewetting length, rivulet width closure, regime scale, gap exchange, contact transfer, bead-covered area at capacity, hydration-gate thresholds, beta_cl tilt exponent, line friction factor.
 - Cross-stack agreement (99 % of clean flags outside the dead band) is untested on a second GPU family or in a browser. The dead-band definition belongs to the inspector; official scoring in a browser is deferred.
 - Fast-tier rank consistency is by construction plus a gate that has not been run. The gate costs about 130 machine-hours of official washes per major version, on top of the benchmark's 536 hours.
 - The arithmetic scripts cited in the document (budget2.py, checks.py, levels.py, arms.py, betam_blend.py) were working files when this was written (they have since been copied to docs/design/scripts/synth/). Their checks should be re-implemented as M13 tests.
 - Staffing by name and a dated plan are not given; section 9 provides tracks and durations only.

The hand-over cap (the reviewer's first option) bounds emitter levels at 3 and 4; raising the cap to 4.6 m/s costs about 5 minutes on the design load and is listed as a use of spare time. The reference tier has 8 levels and no cap.
 - Engineering minor, 2-D or 3-D rate tables for the soil kernel: not adopted. I took the reviewer's alternative of accepting the transcendental calls explicitly (declared per kernel); only the mixture pH is a 2-D table.
 - Engineering blocker item, deriving the S level mix from the r_lim distribution of a golden load: cannot be done before a tracer exists. The budget uses a stated assumption (25 % jet-active) with sensitivities at 15 % and 40 %, and the first full-load trace at MS1 replaces it.

## remainingRisks
 - No kernel of the design has been run. The S rate (0.6 G stage-updates/s) is a floor from the spike's demonstrated arithmetic, not a measurement; the design load has 1.2 minutes of GPU margin at planning rates and reaches 34.7 minutes if S, K, tracing, soil and the jet-active share all come out pessimistic together. The plan then depends on the cut list and variant table of 7.4.
 - The new S flux form and contact-line rule are verified only by a 1-D f64 reference without the capillary term (scratch-revise/s1d.py). Behaviour in 2-D on curved patches, at band boundaries, the pole, corners with several front faces, and in f32 is untested. In 1-D a puddle shoreline with receding angle at or below 5 degrees keeps a residual current of up to 15 mm/s and 0.1 Pa.
 - The hand-over cap of 2.2 m/s clips the momentum of fans cut short and of grazing footprints at their sweep limit; how much S-class wetting this changes is unknown until the tier gate against the uncapped reference tier.
 - The jet-active share of S cell-time (25 %) and the level mix are assumptions; a share of 40 % puts the design load at 24.6 minutes.
 - Official film resolution is coarser than films 12.6 asks: 2 mm on steel, melamine, SAN and copolyester, 1 mm on rivulet-prone plastics in main-wash solves only. Cutlery runs the cascade on narrow planar and stretched-shell patches.
 - The film-class rule in section 3.4 now differs from comments in packages/catalogue/src/types.ts and materials.ts (which say S1 for any receding angle above 10 degrees in main-wash liquor and that cutlery is classified by material alone). I was not allowed to edit other files; those comments need updating. The catalogue's three wetting classes already carry the data the new rule uses.
 - The typical load is now counted from the catalogue (138 items, 4.84 m2) plus two steel pots estimated at 0.22 m2 that the catalogue does not hold; the class split of the 8.5 m2 design load is assumed.
 - Closure choices that remain this document's own and unvalidated: supply factor (sign and size), `liq.wStuck`, jet attenuation under water, underside departure tilt, the transition Reynolds number of the fan boundary layer, the regime-weight thresholds of the S reconstruction, the pulsatility placeholder. Slot nozzles remain bundles of sub-jets.
 - Each liquor class is solved at one temperature; the main-wash record (55 C) is replayed through the cooler heat-up, with S-solved wetting maps flagged for roughly a quarter of the class's time.
 - M11 needs four additions to @spotless/physics that do not exist yet, and the cost of the CPU live-wrench tracer is unmeasured; the shake-down is bounded only by its simulated-time and awake-body allowances until MS0.
 - Film-stage GPU memory is 2.1 GB only if the replay state is evicted during mid-programme exposures (3.4 GB otherwise), and about 4.8 GB of cache files are memory-mapped on a machine that had 5.5 GB free in the spike.
 - The reference-stack plan assumes a second identical machine is obtained and proves bit-identical; until then all campaign hours fall on the one reference laptop or scores are provisional.
 - The document grew from 240 KB to 364 KB. Every number quoted from a scratch script (areas.ts, budget3*.py, closures2.py, s1d.py, tablet.py, wallheat.py, since copied to docs/design/scripts/revise/) must be ported to an M13 test or it can drift from the registry.
 - The onset map of sparsely rained faces has 10 to 30 mm resolution even with pilot-guided parcel allocation, and wall temperatures are up to 11 K high for about 10 s after a step in liquor temperature.
