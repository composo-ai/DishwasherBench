## Lower spray arm (the owner's description, reported)

The owner described the lower arm as having 7 holes and two pill-shaped slots. Two of the holes are
angled backwards (one on each wing) and the other 5 point vertically. One wing has 5 holes, the
other only 3. The pill-shaped slot on the wing with 5 holes is angled slightly radially outward
(the left-hand side of IMG_9082); the pill-shaped slot on the wing with 3 holes is angled slightly
backward, the same way as the hole that is angled backwards. There is also one small hole on the
underside, facing slightly radially inward (IMG_9112); its purpose is not known.

Holes: a 4 mm drill bit is fairly loose and a 5 mm bit does not fit; the owner's estimate is
probably 4.5 mm.

Note: "7 holes" and "5 holes on one wing, 3 on the other" do not add up; the photographs decide the
count.

## Upper spray arm (the owner's description, reported)

The owner described the upper arm as having one hole, four horizontal slots and one vertical slot.
One wing has the hole, two horizontal slots and the vertical slot (upper wing A); the other wing
has three horizontal slots (upper wing B).

- Upper wing A: the hole is vertical, the second slot faces backwards slightly (IMG_9100) and the
  vertical slot faces backwards markedly, perhaps 45 degrees (IMG_9100).
- Upper wing B: the first slot is vertical, the second slot faces backwards and slightly outwards
  (IMG_9104, IMG_9106), and the last slot faces backwards and outwards in the same directions but
  to a greater degree.

Slot sizes were not measured.

Note: "four horizontal slots" and "two on wing A, three on wing B" do not add up; the photographs
decide the count.

Decided that evening: Eco 50 is the programme the game scores on. It is a fixed course, which

harder. Auto 45-65 remains as a secondary programme marked uncalibrated. The first test wash (egg
