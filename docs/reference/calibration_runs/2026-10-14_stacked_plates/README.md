> **Source record.** The note below was written by the machine's owner after the second test
> wash and is kept as written. `IMG_9141.JPG` is the owner's one photograph of how the plates
> were stacked in the lower basket, taken on the morning of 15 October 2026 after the wash; the
> wash is taken to have run the night before. There are no photographs of the plates before or
> after the wash. Asked afterwards how much yolk was used,
> the owner said: "each plate had half a teaspoon"; asked about the programme and the
> detergent: "yep eco50 and finish powerball ultimate". What was made of the wash is in
> `docs/calibration/owner-wash-02-result.md`.

I tested two plates in the same slot, one plate a slot behind, and one plate facing up. Egg yolk air dried for 2 hours. Unbelievably every one came out clean. The one facing up had dirty water on it, but once lightly rinsed there was no egg. I presume there must have been a small gap between the two plates in the same slot, enough for water to drop/channel through - they can't have been completely pressed together. Crazy result, but that's why we do these! I didn't take before/after plate pictures, but I did take a picture of how they were stacked.
