# Music and sound: sources and licences

Every sound file the page ships is listed here. All of them were dedicated to the public domain by
their authors under **CC0 1.0 Universal** (<https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/>),
which allows copying, changing and redistributing the files themselves, for any purpose, without
attribution. The authors are credited all the same, here and on the page (Sound, Credits).

The licence of each work was read on the work's own page on the date given, not taken from the
site's general terms. For every work the page's "License(s)" field says CC0. The description
repeats it for four of the five; the page of Sunset Plains says it in its
"Copyright/Attribution Notice" field instead. The licence is as stated on each track's page; the
project has no other knowledge of how the tracks were made.

The clink of a piece coming to rest and the thud of the door are not files: the page makes them by
synthesis (`apps/web/src/audio/sounds.ts`).

Fetched on 11 October 2026. Each original was checked to be the audio it claims to be (16-bit PCM
WAV, 48 kHz stereo), given one fixed gain to bring it to -18 LUFS
integrated (EBU R128; no compression or other processing), and encoded as MP3, 128 kbit/s, 48 kHz
joint stereo, with all tags removed (`apps/web/scripts/audio/prepare-music.sh`). Nothing else was changed:
the pieces are whole, and the page chooses at run time where to enter, repeat and leave them
(`apps/web/src/audio/manifest.ts`).
